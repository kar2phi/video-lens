Videos for this topic, rated by your agent, with the reason for each. Summarize copies the video-lens command for your agent (or opens Raycast, when set up). In the chat you can also say "summarize 1, 4", "not interested in 3", "mute 6", "more like 2" or "next".

About

video-lens turns a YouTube video into a report you read beside the player: a summary, the takeaway, key points and a timed outline. Your gallery keeps every report you save, and Recommendations suggests what to watch next.
